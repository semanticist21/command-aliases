# Natural writing

Preserve facts, stance, audience, and required terms. Remove repetition, meta-explanation, canned framing, needless headings, fake quotes, and uniform sentence rhythm; prefer concrete verbs and the shortest natural structure. For Korean, avoid translationese, excessive nominalization, stacked modifiers, and mechanical connective endings. Return only the rewrite unless rationale or variants were requested.
