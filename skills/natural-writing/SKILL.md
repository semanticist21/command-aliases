# Natural writing

Choose `draft`, `edit` (default when text is supplied), or `detect`. Detect mode names concrete patterns and quotes evidence without rewriting, scoring authorship, or guessing whether AI wrote it.

For edits, read the whole draft, identify its point and voice signals, then make the minimum effective change. Preserve numbers, dates, names, quotations, causality, negation, conclusions, and formality. Remove empty setup, importance puffery, unsupported attribution, synonym cycling, repetitive rhythm, generic recap endings, and translation-like phrasing only when present. Keep deliberate roughness, humor, uncertainty, and strong opinions.

For Korean, also check excessive or English-style commas, rigid spacing, noun-heavy phrasing, weak verb/adjective variety, unnecessary plurals/pronouns, repetitive connectors, passive forms, and repeated translation-like constructions. Treat them as editing signals, not proof of AI authorship. Read [references/korean-patterns.md](references/korean-patterns.md) when relevant.

Return the revised text and a short change summary unless the user requests text only.
